Who We Are

A Legacy of Excellence in the Built Environment

Architectural and Engineering Services Limited (AESL) is a practicing multidisciplinary firm providing comprehensive architectural, engineering, and surveying solutions for public and private sector clients in the construction industry.

We offer a wide range of services combining architecture, engineering, and surveying to create innovative and sustainable designs that are not only visually appealing but also structurally sound, efficient, and accurately measured.

AESL operates from its head office in Accra, with regional offices across Ghana, bringing professional expertise closer to communities nationwide.
